About Stim_Brain_ARCH_Harness

Stim_Brain_ARCH_Harness is a pioneering project focused on mapping and understanding the electrical signals of the brain within a structured and hardened architectural framework. This project explores the intricate connections between neural activity and computational models, providing a unique perspective on brain function and cognitive processes.

By integrating advanced signal processing techniques with architectural design principles, Stim_Brain_ARCH_Harness offers a comprehensive approach to studying the brain's electrical landscape. This project is ideal for researchers, neuroscientists, and anyone fascinated by the intersection of biology and technology.
